## Observations

### 1. 💡 Medium · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- make a comparison of the two approaches
+ compare the two approaches
```

The direct verb `compare` is the standard technical phrasing; the light-verb `make a comparison of` is grammatical but reads heavier. Learn to prefer the direct verb when one exists.

### 2. 💡 Low · Style — concision

```diff
- the actual best one
+ the best one
```

`best` is already a superlative, so `actual` is usually dropped. Keep it if you want emphasis on "the genuinely best, after comparing."

## Revised Sentence

We should compare the two approaches and choose the best one.
