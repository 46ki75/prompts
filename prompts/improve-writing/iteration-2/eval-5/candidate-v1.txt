## Errors

### 1. ⚠️ Error

```diff
- When the server boot up
+ When the server boots up
```

Subject-verb agreement; `server` is singular.

## Observations

### 1. 💡 Medium — light-verb

```diff
- does the registration of itself to the discovery service
+ registers itself with the discovery service
```

Direct verb `registers` is more concise and idiomatic than the light-verb construction `does the registration`. Also, `with` is the standard preposition for this action.

## Revised Sentence

When the server boots up, it registers itself with the discovery service.
