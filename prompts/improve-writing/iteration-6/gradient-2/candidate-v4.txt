## Observations

### 1. 💡 High · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- doing the migration of the legacy services
+ migrating the legacy services
```

Natives use the direct verb `migrate` rather than the `do the migration of` light-verb construction.

### 2. 💡 High · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- making the improvement of the performance
+ improving the performance
```

`improving the performance` is the standard verbal form; the nominalized `make the improvement of` reads heavy and non-native.

### 3. 💡 High · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- giving the consideration to the reduction of the operational cost
+ considering reducing the operational cost
```

The direct verb `consider` replaces the `give consideration to` light-verb plus a second nominalization (`the reduction of`); chaining both is a clear non-native pattern.

### 4. 💡 High · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- do the notification of all the stakeholders in a manual way
+ manually notify all the stakeholders
```

Natives use the verb `notify` with the adverb `manually`, not the `do the notification of … in a manual way` circumlocution.

### 5. 💡 High · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of the platform
+ lets us modernize the platform
```

`lets us modernize` is the idiomatic verbal form; `gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of` is a non-native nominalized circumlocution.
