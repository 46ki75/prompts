## Naturalness Score

**3/5 — Clear but non-native**

Understandable, but a casual collocation and a wordy relative clause read non-native in a technical/security context. Polish recommended.

## Errors

### 1. ⚠️ Error

```diff
- the roles that is required
+ the roles that are required
```

Subject-verb agreement; `roles` is plural, so it takes `are`.

## Intent Check

No intent concerns.

## Observations

### 1. 💡 High · Idiom — collocation

```diff
- to finish a job
+ to perform a task
```

`perform a task` is the standard technical collocation when describing least privilege; `finish a job` reads casual and non-native here.

### 2. 💡 Medium · Idiom — concision

```diff
- assign only the roles that are required
+ assign only the roles required
```

The participial `roles required` is tighter and standard in security docs.

### 3. 💡 Low · Style — register

```diff
- You must assign
+ Assign
```

Technical docs often use a direct imperative. Keep `You must` if you want to stress the obligation.

## Revised Sentence

Assign only the roles required to perform a task.
