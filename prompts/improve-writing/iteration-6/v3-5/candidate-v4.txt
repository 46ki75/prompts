## Observations

### 1. 💡 Medium · Idiom — light-verb

```diff
- make a comparison of the two approaches
+ compare the two approaches
```

Natives prefer the direct verb `compare` over the `make a comparison of` light-verb construction; it reads tighter and more idiomatic.

### 2. 💡 Low · Style — concision

```diff
- the actual best one
+ the best one
```

`actual` is often dropped in technical writing. Keep it if you want to emphasize the *genuinely* best option.

## Revised Sentence
