## Errors

### 1. ⚠️ Error

## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — collocation

```diff
- to finish a job
+ to perform a task
```

`perform a task` is the standard technical collocation; `finish a job` reads colloquially.

### 2. 💡 Medium — concision


Participial `roles required` is tighter and standard in access-control docs.

Technical docs often use direct imperatives.
