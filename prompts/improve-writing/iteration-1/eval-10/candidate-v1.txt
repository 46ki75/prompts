## Observations

### 1. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- The actual real reason
+ The real reason
```

`actual` and `real` are near-synonyms here; natives use one, not both.

### 2. 💡 Low — collocation

```diff
- the real reason of the failure
+ the real reason for the failure
```

`reason for X` is the standard collocation; `reason of` sounds non-native.

### 3. 💡 Low — concision

```diff
- the disk was totally full
+ the disk was full
```

`full` is absolute, so `totally` adds little; natives often drop it.

## Revised Sentence

The real reason for the failure is that the disk was full.
