## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we are making the improvement of the performance
+ we are improving performance
```

The nominalized `making the improvement of` is a classic non-native pattern; natives use the direct verb `improving`.

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- giving the consideration to the reduction of the operational cost
+ looking at reducing operational costs
```

Stacked nominalizations (`giving consideration to`, `the reduction of`) sound markedly non-native; the verb forms are far more idiomatic.

### 3. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the notification of all the stakeholders in a manual way
+ we notify all stakeholders manually
```

`do the notification of … in a manual way` should collapse to the verb `notify` plus the adverb `manually`.

### 4. 💡 Medium — light-verb

```diff
- This approach gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of the platform
+ This approach lets us modernize the platform
```

`gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of` is a chain of nominalizations; `lets us modernize` is the natural phrasing.

### 5. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- At the present moment, we are
+ Right now, we are
```

`At the present moment` is wordy; natives prefer `Right now` or simply drop it.
