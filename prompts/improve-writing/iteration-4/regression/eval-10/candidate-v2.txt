## Observations

### 1. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- The actual real reason
+ The real reason
```

`actual` and `real` are near-synonyms here; natives use one, not both.

### 2. 💡 Medium — collocation

```diff
- the disk was totally full
+ the disk was completely full
```

`full` is an absolute, so `completely full` is the standard collocation; `totally` reads more casual and less idiomatic with it.

## Revised Sentence

The real reason for the failure is that the disk was completely full.
