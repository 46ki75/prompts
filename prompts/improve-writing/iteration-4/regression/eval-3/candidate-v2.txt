## Errors

## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — register / collocation

```diff
- to finish a job
+ to perform a task
```

`perform a task` is the standard technical collocation; `finish a job` reads casually.

### 2. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- assign only the roles that are required

```

Participial `roles required` is tighter and standard in security/IAM docs.

### 3. 💡 Low — register

```diff

+ Assign
```

Technical docs often use direct imperatives instead of `You must`.
