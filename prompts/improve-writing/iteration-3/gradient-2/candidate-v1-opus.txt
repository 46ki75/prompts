## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — concision

```diff
- we are making the improvement of the performance
+ we are improving performance
```

Natives prefer the direct verb over the nominalization "making the improvement of."

### 2. 💡 High — concision

```diff
- giving the consideration to the reduction of the operational cost
+ looking at reducing operational cost
```

"Giving the consideration to the reduction of" stacks nominalizations; natives use direct verbs.

### 3. 💡 High — collocation

```diff
- we do the notification of all the stakeholders in a manual way
+ we notify all the stakeholders manually
```

"Do the notification of ... in a manual way" is non-native; the verb "notify" plus the adverb "manually" is standard.

### 4. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- our team is doing the migration of the legacy services
+ our team is migrating the legacy services
```

The light-verb construction "doing the migration of" is wordier than the direct verb "migrating."

### 5. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of the platform
+ lets us modernize the platform
```

"Gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of" is a stacked nominalization; natives use the direct verb.
