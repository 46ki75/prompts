## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the checking of the configuration
+ we check the configuration
```

Native English avoids "do + noun" constructions in favor of the direct verb. "Check" is simpler and clearer.

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the restart of the services
+ we restart the services
```

"Restart the services" is the standard phrasing; "do the restart of" is non-native padding.

### 3. 💡 High — preposition / collocation

```diff
- for the achievement of a clean state
+ to achieve a clean state
```

Native phrasing uses the infinitive `to achieve` rather than the prepositional "for the achievement of" construction.

### 4. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we also do the notification of the team
+ we also notify the team
```

Direct verb `notify` is standard; "do the notification of" is non-native bureaucratic padding.

### 5. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- about the completion of the process
+ that the process is complete
```

"Notify the team that X" is more direct than "notify about the completion of the process."

## Revised Sentence

During deployment, we check the configuration, restart the services to achieve a clean state, and notify the team that the process is complete.
