## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the checking of the configuration
+ we check the configuration
```

The "do the checking of" construction is non-native; natives use the direct verb "check".

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the restart of the services
+ we restart the services
```

Same pattern: "do the restart of" should collapse to the verb "restart".

### 3. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we also do the notification of the team
+ we also notify the team
```

"do the notification of" reads as non-native; the verb "notify" is standard.

### 4. 💡 Medium — concision

```diff
- for the achievement of a clean state
+ to reach a clean state
```

Natives prefer an infinitive of purpose over the nominalized "for the achievement of".

### 5. 💡 Medium — collocation

```diff
- about the completion of the process
+ that the process is complete
```

A finite clause reads more naturally than the nominalized "about the completion of".

## Revised Sentence

At deployment time, we check the configuration, then restart the services to reach a clean state, and we also notify the team that the process is complete.
