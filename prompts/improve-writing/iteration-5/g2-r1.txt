## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- making the improvement of the performance
+ improving performance
```

Natives use the direct verb `improve` rather than the `make the improvement of` light-verb construction.

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- giving the consideration to the reduction of the operational cost
+ looking at reducing operational cost
```

`reduce X` (or `look at reducing X`) is the standard verbal form; `give the consideration to the reduction of` is a heavy non-native circumlocution.

### 3. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the notification of all the stakeholders in a manual way
+ we notify all stakeholders manually
```

Natives use the direct verb `notify` plus the adverb `manually` instead of `do the notification of ... in a manual way`.

### 4. 💡 High — collocation

```diff
- At the present moment, we are
+ Right now, we are
```

`at the present moment` is an overformal circumlocution; `right now` or `currently` is the idiomatic choice.

### 5. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of the platform
+ lets us modernize the platform
```

`lets us modernize` uses direct verbs; `gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of` stacks nominalizations and reads markedly non-native.
