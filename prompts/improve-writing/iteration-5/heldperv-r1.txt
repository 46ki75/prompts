## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- make the configuration of the servers
+ configure the servers
```

Natives use the direct verb `configure` rather than the `make the configuration of` light-verb construction.

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff

```

`install the packages` is the standard verbal form; the nominalized `the installation of` reads heavy and non-native.

### 3. 💡 High — light-verb


Natives use the direct verb `verify`; `do the verification of` is a non-native circumlocution.

### 4. 💡 High — light-verb


`notify the operators` is the idiomatic verbal form; `give the notification to` reads as a non-native paraphrase.

`First,` is the standard sequencing marker in technical writing; `In the beginning,` reads as narrative rather than procedural.
