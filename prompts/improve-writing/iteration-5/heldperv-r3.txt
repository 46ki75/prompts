## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we make the configuration of the servers and the installation of the packages
+ we configure the servers and install the packages
```

Natives use the direct verbs `configure` and `install` rather than the `make the configuration of` / `the installation of` light-verb constructions.

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the verification of the connections
+ we verify the connections
```

`verify the connections` is the standard verbal form; the nominalized `do the verification of` reads heavy and non-native.

### 3. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- give the notification to the operators about the readiness of the system
+ notify the operators that the system is ready
```

`notify the operators` is the idiomatic verb; `give the notification to` is a non-native circumlocution and `the readiness of the system` nominalizes what natives express as `the system is ready`.

### 4. 💡 Medium — collocation

```diff
- In the beginning, we
+ First, we
```

For a sequence of steps, natives open with `First,`; `In the beginning` evokes narrative or origin rather than a procedure.

### 5. 💡 Low — concision

```diff
- After that, we
+ Then we
```

`Then` is tighter and standard when chaining steps; `After that` is acceptable but wordier.

## Revised Sentence

First, we configure the servers and install the packages. Then we verify the connections and notify the operators that the system is ready.
