## Observations

### 1. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we are making the improvement of the performance
+ we are improving performance
```

Natives use the direct verb `improve` rather than the `make the improvement of` light-verb construction.

### 2. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- giving the consideration to the reduction of the operational cost
+ considering how to reduce operational cost
```

`considering` (with the direct verb `reduce`) is the idiomatic form; the stacked nominalizations `giving the consideration to` and `the reduction of` read heavy and non-native.

### 3. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- we do the notification of all the stakeholders in a manual way
+ we notify all stakeholders manually
```

The direct verb `notify` plus the adverb `manually` is standard; `do the notification of ... in a manual way` is a non-native circumlocution.

### 4. 💡 High — light-verb

```diff
- This approach gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of the platform
+ This approach lets us modernize the platform
```

`lets us modernize` uses the direct verb; `gives us the possibility to achieve the modernization of` is a chain of nominalizations that reads non-native.

### 5. 💡 Medium — collocation

```diff
- At the present moment, we are
+ Currently, we are
```

`Currently` (or `Right now`) is the standard technical collocation; `At the present moment` is wordy and uncommon.
