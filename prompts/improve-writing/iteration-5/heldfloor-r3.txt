## Naturalness Score

**4/5 — Near-native**

Two tense slips aside, the underlying phrasing is native with one collocation choice that could be more idiomatic. Polish is optional.

## Errors

### 1. ⚠️ Error

```diff
- the pipeline run twice
+ the pipeline ran twice
```

Past tense; `yesterday` sets a past context, so `run` should be `ran`.

### 2. ⚠️ Error

```diff
- and produce no error
+ and produced no error
```

Tense agreement; the action is in the past, so `produce` should be `produced`.

## Observations

### 1. 💡 Medium — collocation

```diff
- produced no error
+ produced no errors
```

When reporting outcomes, natives use the plural `no errors` to mean "zero errors of any kind"; the singular `no error` reads slightly off here.

## Revised Sentence

Yesterday the pipeline ran twice and produced no errors.
