Yes — Toasty is an ORM (object-relational mapper) for Rust, started by Carl Lerche (the author of Tokio, Bytes, Mio, and other core Rust async libraries). It was announced/open-sourced relatively recently and is designed to work with both SQL databases and NoSQL stores like DynamoDB, with an async-first API.

- This comes from my training data (cutoff January 2026), not from a freshly verified source, since I have no retrieval tools available in this run.
