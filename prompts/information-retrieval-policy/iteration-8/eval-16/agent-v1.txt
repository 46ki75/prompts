The 2008 financial crisis is usually dated by two milestones:

- Onset (2007): The crisis began as turmoil in the US subprime mortgage market. A commonly cited early trigger is August 2007, when BNP Paribas froze redemptions on funds exposed to subprime assets, signaling broad credit-market stress. Signs appeared even earlier in 2007 with the failure of subprime lenders and the collapse of two Bear Stearns hedge funds.

- Acute systemic phase (September 2008): The crisis reached its peak with the bankruptcy of Lehman Brothers on September 15, 2008, which triggered a global financial panic.

So the answer depends on what you mean by "start": the underlying crisis built through 2007, while the dramatic global phase most people associate with "2008" began in September 2008.
